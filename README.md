# **Cybersecurity-Portfolio**
## **Introduction**
I am a former naval nuclear reactor operator and an aspiring cybersecurity professional. Over my 10 years in the Navy, I served in Yokosuka, Japan; San Diego, California; and Beaumont, Texas. Along the way, I graduated from the Naval Nuclear Power Training Program with honors, troubleshot critical instrumentation and control equipment associated with reactor safety, operated nuclear reactors aboard deployed aircraft carriers, ran a large Navy recruiting station, and saw the world.

Now, I’m ready to start a new chapter in life; the chapter of cybersecurity. In order to break into the field, I’m pursuing a Bachelor of Applied Cybersecurity from the SANS Technology Institute, earning GIAC certifications, completing cybersecurity internships, and competing in capture-the-flag (CTF) competitions. I realize cybersecurity is a competitive field, but that works out, because I'm a competitive person.

I’ll use this repository to showcase that hard work and show future employers what I can do.
## Capture-the-Flag Experience
I competed in the Fall 2026 National Cyber League CTF competition. I completed challenges in the areas of OSINT, Cryptography, Password Cracking, Log Analysis, Network Traffic Analysis, Forensics, Scanning and Reconnaissance, Exploitation and Enumeration, and Web Application Exploitation. I completed 86.5% of these challenges, which put me well within the top 10% of all competitors.
